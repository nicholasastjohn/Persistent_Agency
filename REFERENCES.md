## Most important 2026 novelty constraints

The paper should not claim novelty for continuous cognitive cycles, global-workspace framing, heterogeneous components, hierarchical memory, agent harnesses, procedural memory, or long-horizon state management individually. The proposed contribution is the conjunction and interaction of recurrent endogenous cognition, asymmetric primitive control, explicit reactive bypass, procedural automaticity, offline consolidation, reconstructive memory, and plastic routing across timescales.
